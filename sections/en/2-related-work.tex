\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Fluid-assisted evolutionary search for the MFJSP}\label{sec:related-fluid}
\pending{The fluid master--slave evolutionary algorithm and its variants relax the MFJSP to a continuous fluid model and use
the resulting flow ratios for initialization, dispatching rules or macro-level guidance of a master population
\citeneed{fluid master--slave EA for the MFJSP, 2022, and its setup-time variant}.} These works establish that fluid
ratios carry a meaningful flow structure, but the fluid solution stays a prior: the search and any learning component still
face the full discrete problem, and the representation remains job-indexed.

\subsection{Fluid models combined with reinforcement learning}\label{sec:related-rl}
\pending{Multi-policy PPO with fluid quantities as state features \citeneed{MPPPO, 2024}, PPO that tunes the parameters of a
fluid EA on line \citeneed{DSLFMAE, 2025}, and fluid relaxation feeding a double DQN for dynamic multiplicity scheduling
\citeneed{fluid + DDQN, 2025} use fluid quantities as features or control signals.} None of them decomposes the source of the
gap. \pending{A concurrent line studies the exact fluid reachability of the limit of infinitely many copies
\citeneed{recursive fluid reachability, 2026}}, which is complementary to the finite and unbalanced multiplicity range
studied here.

\subsection{Learning to improve and learning to filter candidates}\label{sec:related-improve}
Improvement-type deep reinforcement learning for the JSP and FJSP lets a policy network take the complete improvement
decision and relies on credit assignment \citeneed{L2S-type critical-block improvement policies, 2024; memory-augmented
improvement heuristics for the FJSP, 2025}. Supervised candidate filtering, as in NeuroLKH's candidate edge sets and learned
move screening for tabu search \citeneed{NeuroLKH; learned tabu move filtering}, is the methodological ancestor of the ranker
used here, but it has not been combined with a relaxation-based decomposition. Classical FJSP metaheuristics supply the
host components: the two-layer genetic encoding \cite{pezzella2008genetic}, critical-block tabu search
\cite{mastrolilli2000effective} and the Brandimarte \cite{brandimarte1993routing} and Hurink \cite{hurink1994tabu}
benchmarks.
